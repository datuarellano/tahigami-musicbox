                What happens during an update?
                
    
                    
  1. The Music Box is rebooted into its built-in bootloader.
    2. 
                    
  2. The new firmware is downloaded, checked against a checksum, and written.
    3. 
                    
  3. The device restarts on its own and starts playing again.
